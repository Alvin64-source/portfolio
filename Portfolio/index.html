The problem: the hostel's paper visitor log made it impossible to know who was on-site, who they were visiting, or who'd overstayed.

What I built: a front-desk system that checks visitors in/out, links each visit to the resident they're seeing, flags overstays live, autofills returning visitors, and exports the log as CSV — no backend required.

What I'd improve next: swap the localStorage model for a real backend, so two front desks can see the same live log.
