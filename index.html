01 / The person behind the pixels

Technical enough to build it. Curious enough to make it better.

I like work where practical problem-solving meets creative thinking. I don't just want to know how something works. I want to make it useful.

I'm an IT graduate-in-progress from the Catholic University of Eastern Africa (CUEA), based in Nairobi, Kenya. My foundation is in IT support and systems, with hands-on practice building responsive websites and browser-based apps.

My projects range from a hostel visitor check-in system and an election platform to a website for a community organisation, a car-rental concept, and small AI-powered product experiments. Some are shipped, some are still evolving, and I label them honestly.

I completed an industrial attachment at Tourism Fund, rotating through ICT and several business departments. It gave me a wider view of how technology supports real teams, operations, and services.
